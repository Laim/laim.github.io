## About Me
### The longer version

I started tinkering with computers and consoles ([PS3](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=PlayStation_3&oldid=1351272495)) back in 2008, mostly breaking them at that point. The first programming language I learned was [Visual Basic 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Visual_Basic_(classic)&oldid=1340478897), as that was the only book I had available to me, before moving onto [VB.NET](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Visual_Basic_(.NET)&oldid=1345806817) sometime in 2013-ish. Around the time I was learning Visual Basic 6, I was also getting more interested in how websites worked and started learning [PHP](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=PHP&oldid=1352067195), which I used near enough every day until 2022, before stepping away from web development for a while.

In 2020/2021 I switched from VB.NET to [C#](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=C_Sharp_(programming_language)&oldid=1347180106) and started working on my own game, which I released on Steam called [The Lonely Helmet](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1730310/The_Lonely_Helmet/). It was not great. Around this time, I also created the first version of my own [Game Tracker](https://laim.scot/blog/i-made-a-game-tracker), which I have been using every day since and recently upgraded from an unmaintainable PHP project to ASP.NET with Razor. In my free time, I also like to build physical things around the house or in my garden, of various quality and success.

Outside of my personal life, I work at SoftwareOne as a Senior ITAM Consultant. I started my work career at Getronics as an Apprentice in Software Asset Management, before moving to Softcat in 2018 where I created quite a few [custom tools](http://github.com/goosetuv) to make my life easier while managing customer Snow License Manager estates. I moved to SoftwareOne in 2022 after getting burnout from COVID-19 and needing a change, where I've been since.

If you have any questions or just want a chat, you can [contact me](/contact) in various places.
